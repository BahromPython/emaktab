The Problem

In Tajikistan, quality education
depends on your postcode

Students in the capital, Dushanbe, have access to private tutors, prep courses, and quality resources. Students in rural districts — Murghab, Ishkoshim, Vanj — have none of that.

No private tutors outside cities
Most rural families cannot afford or access private tutoring. National exam preparation (ВОА) is critical for university entrance — and unavailable for millions of students.
No Tajik-language digital resources
Existing EdTech platforms are in Russian, English, or Uzbek — not Tajik. Students who learn in Tajik have been effectively excluded from the digital education revolution.
"The quality of a student's education in Tajikistan is determined almost entirely by the city they were born in. eMaktab exists to change that."
The Platform

What eMaktab is

A complete, free, Tajik-language school platform — covering the full national curriculum from grade 1 to grade 11.

📚
Full curriculum
3,574 structured lessons across 79 subjects, aligned to the Tajikistan national school curriculum for grades 1 through 11.
🤖
AI tutor in Tajik
A conversational AI tutor that answers student questions in the Tajik language — the first of its kind for Tajikistan's 9 million people.
📶
Works offline
Designed to work in low-connectivity rural areas. Students can access lessons even with intermittent or no internet connection.
🎓
National exam prep (ВОА)
Dedicated preparation for the national university entrance exam — the gateway to higher education for every Tajik student.
📱
Mobile-first
Most students in Tajikistan access the internet via smartphone. eMaktab is fully responsive and installable as an app on any phone.
💰
Free forever
No subscription, no paywall, no ads. Built entirely by one developer on a zero budget, and committed to staying free for every student.
AI Tutor

The first AI tutor
in the Tajik language

Every student deserves a tutor who speaks their language. eMaktab's AI answers questions, explains concepts, and helps students work through problems — in Tajik.

How it works

Ask anything.
Get an answer in Tajik.

Students type any question about their lesson — a math problem, a grammar rule, a history event — and the AI responds clearly in Tajik. It remembers the conversation context, so follow-up questions work naturally.


Available from any device, no login required for basic access. The AI is powered by the Gemini model, routed through a secure server-side proxy so no API keys are ever exposed to the student.

The Story

Built by one person,
for an entire country

eMaktab was designed, built, and maintained entirely by Bahrom Ashurov — a student from Tajikistan with no team, no funding, and no institutional backing.

Every lesson was curated. Every subject was structured. The platform was built from scratch as a single-page web application, with an offline-first architecture so it works even where internet is unreliable.

The AI tutor — the first to respond in the Tajik language — was integrated as a secure server-side proxy, so students in rural schools can ask questions without exposing any API credentials.

The goal is simple: every student in Tajikistan, regardless of where they live or how much their family earns, should have access to the same quality of education.
